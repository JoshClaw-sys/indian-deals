What actually matters in a 1.5-ton AC under ₹35,000

Five years ago, ₹35,000 bought you a fixed-speed, aluminium-coil 3-star AC that hummed loudly, hit set temperature in 25 minutes, and cost ₹500/month in electricity. In 2026, the same ₹35,000 gets you an inverter split AC with copper coils, ISEER 4.0+, convertible cooling modes, and a 10-year compressor warranty. Indian AC brands have caught up with global quality at budget prices -- but the spread between the best and the worst is wide, and the wrong pick will cost you ₹1,500+ extra per year in electricity.

We compared 20+ 1.5-ton inverter split ACs under ₹35,000 across cooling consistency, ISEER rating, noise levels, coil type, convertible modes, and compressor warranty. Here's what we found -- and one popular 5-star model that you should skip despite the high ISEER rating.

Five specs decide whether a 1.5-ton AC under ₹35,000 is actually a good deal in 2026.

ISEER above 3.8 is the floor. ISEER (Indian Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio) measures real-world cooling efficiency across a full Indian summer -- a BEE 3-star AC typically has ISEER 3.8-4.0; a 5-star hits 4.5-5.0. The difference in electricity is roughly 25-30%. On a 1.5-ton AC running 8 hours/day for 5 months, a 5-star AC saves ₹1,500-2,000/year vs a 3-star. The upfront premium for 5-star is ₹3,000-5,000 -- payback in 2-3 years.

Copper coils, always. Aluminium coils are cheaper and lighter but corrode in Indian humidity within 4-5 years -- especially in coastal cities (Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata). Copper coils last 10-15 years with annual servicing. Every AC in this guide has copper coils; skip aluminium-coil ACs even at lower prices.

Convertible mode is worth ₹1,500+ premium. Convertible ACs let you run the compressor at 50-75% capacity for shoulder seasons (October-November, February-March) when full cooling is overkill. Saves 20-30% electricity in those months. Brands call this differently: Voltas '4-in-1 convertible', LG 'AI Convertible', Blue Star '5-in-1 convertible'. They're the same feature.

10-year compressor warranty is the right benchmark. Compressor replacement costs ₹8,000-12,000 + labour. Voltas, LG, Daikin, Blue Star, Carrier, Hitachi all offer 10-year compressor warranty as standard in 2026. Brands offering 1-year compressor warranty are a red flag -- the compressor is the only part that fails catastrophically, and a short warranty means the brand doesn't trust their own product.

Skip non-inverter ACs in 2026. Fixed-speed ACs are obsolete for residential use -- they consume 30-40% more electricity and have higher compressor wear from constant start-stop cycles. Every AC in this guide is inverter. If you see a 'non-inverter' label on a listing, skip it.

Frequently asked questions

Is 1.5-ton the right size for an Indian bedroom?

For rooms 120-180 sq ft, yes. Below 120 sq ft, a 1-ton AC is enough and cheaper to run. Above 180 sq ft, you'll need a 2-ton AC for proper cooling. 1.5-ton is the sweet spot for master bedrooms in most Indian flats.

What is ISEER and why does it matter?

ISEER (Indian Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio) measures AC efficiency across a full Indian summer. Higher ISEER = lower electricity bills. BEE 3-star ACs typically have ISEER 3.8-4.0; 5-star ACs hit 4.5-5.0. A 5-star AC saves roughly ₹1,500-2,000 per year vs a 3-star.

Do I need an inverter AC under ₹35,000?

Yes. Fixed-speed ACs in 2026 are obsolete for residential use -- they consume 30-40% more electricity and have higher compressor wear. Every AC in this guide is inverter. The 'non-inverter' label on older listings is a red flag -- skip.

Copper coil vs aluminium coil -- which is better?

Copper. Aluminium coils are cheaper but corrode faster in Indian humidity, especially in coastal cities. Every AC in this list has copper coils. Avoid aluminium-coil ACs even at lower prices.

How long do split ACs last under ₹35,000?

With annual servicing, 10-12 years. The compressor is the most expensive part to replace (₹8,000-12,000), so brands with 10-year compressor warranty (Voltas, LG, Daikin) are the right picks. Brands with 1-year comprehensive + 5-year compressor warranty are acceptable but not ideal.
