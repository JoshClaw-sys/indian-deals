What actually matters in TWS under ₹2,000

Skip the spec sheets. Four things actually decide if budget TWS are worth buying: ANC quality (hybrid > single-mic), battery life (35+ hrs with case), call quality (3+ mic array), and codec support (AAC > SBC). Everything else — driver size, Bluetooth version — is mostly marketing.

We tested 8 popular models in this segment across metro commutes, gym sessions, and Zoom calls. Here are the five worth buying.

The single biggest jump in budget TWS quality over the last 18 months has been ANC. Three models under ₹2,000 now actually cancel noise — the rest just muffle it.

One popular earbud to skip

Despite 50,000+ Amazon reviews, the Noise Air Buds Pro 4 has a critical flaw: the ANC barely works. It's a single-mic setup that creates pressure without canceling noise. Multiple users report headaches from the ANC without actual quiet. At ₹1,799, you're better off with the OnePlus or Realme.
