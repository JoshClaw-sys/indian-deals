What actually matters in a grill microwave under 15,000

A grill microwave is the right pick for Indian kitchens that reheat and cook daily but rarely bake. At the under-15,000 band, manufacturers differentiate on grill output wattage, cavity size, child lock, and ease of cleaning. Most buyers never use the full preset menu; a handful of well-tuned manual modes plus a high-wattage grill element cover almost every Indian use case.

The grill element is what makes a grill microwave a grill microwave. A separate heating coil sits above the cavity, typically 1,000-1,200 W. The coil browns paneer, kebabs, papad, and bread while the magnetron simultaneously heats food from inside. Look for grill ratings of 1,000 W and above; below that, food steams instead of crisping.

Cavity size matters more than buyers expect. A 20-litre cavity fits a standard dinner plate with 1 cm of room around the edges. A 23-litre cavity fits a casserole, two dinner plates side by side, and most Indian tiffin sizes. Families of 4 should not go below 20 litres; singles or couples can accept 17-19 litres.

Cleaning is a recurring pain point. Cavities with anti-bacterial coatings or steam-clean cycles stay cleaner longer. Stainless steel interiors last but show grease. Painted interiors clean easily but stain over years. Most 2026 models ship an anti-bacterial cavity that combines the durability of stainless steel with the cleanability of paint.

Child lock is the safety feature Indian households want most. A press-and-hold-to-lock button deactivates the control panel until the same gesture is repeated. All four recommendations below include child lock by default.

Preset menus are mostly marketing. Buyers usually only use 4-6 menus: reheat, defrost, rice, dal, pizza/papad, and beverage. Pick a model whose control layout makes the 5 modes you use most easy to reach. Avoid models that bury common modes inside three-level sub-menus.

Frequently asked questions

Can a grill microwave make naan or roti?

Grill microwaves can warm and lightly crisp pre-made naan and roti. They cannot replicate the open-flame tandoor texture of freshly made naan. For at-home naan that browns evenly, a convection microwave with a baking rack is the better tool.

What is the difference between grill and convection microwaves?

A grill microwave has a heating coil at the top of the cavity that crisps and browns. A convection microwave adds a fan-forced heating element that circulates hot air, enabling baking, roasting and crisping from all sides. Convection is the more versatile technology for Indian baking use cases.

Is 20 litres enough for a family of 4?

Yes, for typical Indian reheating and cooking: dal, sabzi, rice, paneer tikka, papad. Not enough for large baking dishes or whole chicken in a single shot. Families that regularly cook for 4+ should consider 23-25 litres.

Are microwave presets really useful?

For specific common dishes like paneer tikka, dal, rice and beverages, a tuned preset saves the guesswork. For one-off dishes, manual mode with timed power level is more flexible. Most buyers eventually use 4-6 presets regularly.

How long do microwave ovens last?

Expect 7-10 years of daily use. The magnetron has a finite operating life, but it lasts longer than most buyers expect when the cavity is kept clean and the door seal is intact. Avoid slamming the door; that's the most common cause of early failure.
