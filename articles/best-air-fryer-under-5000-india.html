What actually matters in an air fryer under ₹5,000

Skip the marketing. Three specs decide if an air fryer actually works: wattage (1200W+ for crisp), basket design (mesh > solid), and temperature range (up to 200°C). Everything else is fluff.

We tested six models in this price range across fries, chicken wings, and frozen samosas. Here's what we found — and one model you should avoid even though it has 10,000 Amazon reviews.

If you only buy one thing from this list: get the Philips HD9200/90. It's the only one under ₹5,000 with rapid air technology that doesn't leave you with soggy bottoms.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Are cheap air fryers worth it?

Yes, if you pick right. The sweet spot in India is ₹3,500–₹5,000 — below ₹3,000, heating is uneven and build quality drops.

What size air fryer do I need?

3-4L for 1-2 people, 5-6L for a family of 4, 6L+ if you cook in batches or for guests.

Do air fryers use a lot of electricity?

A 1300W fryer running 20 minutes uses about 0.4 units — roughly ₹4-6 per use on most Indian tariff plans.

Can I bake in an air fryer?

Yes — most modern air fryers handle cookies, small cakes, and even grilled sandwiches.
