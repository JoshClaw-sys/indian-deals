Solo vs grill vs convection: pick by cooking style

Microwaves come in three modes:

Solo microwaves (Rs 4,000-7,000) only generate microwave energy for heating. They reheat food, defrost frozen items, and cook basic recipes like Maggi and rice. They cannot bake, grill, or roast. Fine for bachelors; limiting for families.

Grill microwaves (Rs 7,000-12,000) add a heating element at the top for grilling and toasting. They handle tandoori chicken, kebabs, grilled sandwiches, and toast. They cannot bake cakes (no fan-forced heater).

Convection microwaves (Rs 11,000-25,000) add a fan-forced heater that circulates hot air. They handle baking (cakes, cookies, pizzas, bread), roasting (chicken, vegetables), and grilling. They are the right pick for Indian families who bake even once a month.

For most Indian buyers in 2026, convection is the default. The picks below are all convection microwaves. If you are certain you will only reheat and make Maggi, a solo microwave like the Bajaj 2005ETB (Rs 5,490) is enough -- but you lose the option to bake, grill, or roast.

Frequently asked questions

Solo, grill, or convection -- which do I need?

Solo microwaves only heat (reheat, defrost, basic cooking). Grill microwaves add a heating element for tandoori, kebabs, and toast. Convection microwaves add a fan-forced heater for baking cakes, pizzas, and roasting. For Indian kitchens, convection is the right pick if you bake even once a month; grill is fine if you only do tandoori; solo is enough if you only reheat and make Maggi. The picks below are all convection.

What capacity do I need for an Indian family?

20-23L for bachelors and couples. 25-28L for a family of 3-4. 30L+ for a joint family or if you bake large cakes. Indian thalis fit comfortably up to 28L. Most convection microwaves under Rs 20,000 come in 23-28L, which is the sweet spot.

Is inverter microwave worth the premium?

Yes if you cook 2+ hours daily. Inverter microwaves deliver consistent power at low settings (instead of cycling full-power on/off like conventional microwaves). This is critical for melting chocolate, softening butter, and slow-cooking dal. The premium is Rs 1,500-3,000 over non-inverter and the cooking quality difference is obvious.

How long do microwaves last?

8-12 years for the magnetron (the heating element) with normal use. The control boards and turntable motors last 5-8 years. LG, IFB, Samsung, and Panasonic all have authorised service centres in 200+ Indian cities. Buy from a brand with a service centre in your city -- a microwave that cannot be repaired is a Rs 15,000 paperweight in year 8.

Can a convection microwave replace an OTG?

For most Indian home baking, yes. A convection microwave + grill rack + 30L capacity handles cakes, cookies, pizzas, and grilled vegetables. An OTG (oven-toaster-griller) is better for toast, croissants, and high-heat roasting (above 220 degrees). If you bake 2-3 times a week, a convection microwave is enough; if you bake daily or run a home bakery, an OTG or built-in oven is better.
