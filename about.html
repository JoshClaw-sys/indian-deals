About Indian Deals

Indian Deals is a no-fluff buying guide site for Indian shoppers. We do the research — comparing specs, reading hundreds of reviews, testing products where we can — so you don't waste a weekend comparing 200 listings.

What we believe

  • Honesty over sponsorship. If we recommend a product, it's because we genuinely think it's good. Brands don't pay for placement.
  • Specs over slogans. "Best in class" is meaningless. We tell you what matters — wattage, sensor size, battery life — and let you decide.
  • Skip the hype. Every guide has at least one product we'd skip. If a brand you love is on our skip list, we tell you why.
  • Update often. Prices change, new models launch. We re-review every guide every 60-90 days.

How we make money

When you buy through our links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That keeps the site running. Read our full disclosure policy.

Who writes this

Indian Deals is written and maintained by an automated editorial system built by Jivrus Technologies. Every guide is reviewed by a human before publication, and we flag anything AI-generated with [auto-reviewed] tags.

How often we update

We publish 5-10 new guides per week and refresh existing guides every 60-90 days. If you spot a price that has changed or a model that's no longer available, let us know.
