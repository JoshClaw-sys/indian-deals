What actually matters in a microwave under ₹15,000

A ₹15,000 microwave covers solo, grill, and convection — all three. Five things decide if it's worth buying: capacity (20L is the right size for Indian families of 3-4, 23-25L for 5+), convection vs grill only (convection = can bake, grill only = can only reheat/cook/grill), wattage (800-900W is standard), inverter tech (lighter + more even cooking), and auto-cook menus (Indian-specific menus are a real time-saver). For Indian cooking, convection is essential — tandoori chicken, cakes, and bread need it.

For most Indian families, a 20-23L convection microwave with at least 30 Indian auto-cook menus is the right shape. Below 20L is too small for a family of 4. Above 25L is overkill for daily use.

If you only buy one thing from this list: the LG 20L Convection Microwave (MC2146BP) — best balance of brand reliability, Indian auto-cook menus, and price under ₹15,000.

Solo vs grill vs convection: what you actually need

This is the most common confusion. Each type does different things:

  • Solo — Reheat, defrost, basic cooking. No grilling, no baking. Cheapest (₹5,000-8,000). Buy only if you only reheat and defrost.
  • Grill — Solo + grilling. Can make tandoori, kebabs, grilled chicken. No baking. Mid-range (₹8,000-12,000). Buy if you want grilling but not baking.
  • Convection — Solo + grill + bake. Can make cakes, bread, pizza, tandoori, everything. Most expensive (₹12,000-25,000). Buy if you want a real all-in-one.

For Indian cooking, convection is the right call. The price difference (₹3,000-5,000 over grill) is worth it for the versatility.

Frequently asked questions

20L vs 23L vs 25L: which size?

Single/couple: 17-20L. Family of 3-4: 20-23L. Family of 5+: 23-25L. Joint family (7+): 28L+. Most Indian families of 3-4 should pick 20-23L.

Convection vs OTG: which is better for baking?

OTG (Oven Toaster Griller) is dedicated to baking — better for serious bakers (cakes, bread, cookies). Convection microwave does everything (reheat, cook, grill, bake) but is less consistent for serious baking. For all-in-one: convection microwave. For dedicated baking: OTG.

How long does a microwave last?

8-10 years for LG/Samsung/IFB. 5-8 years for cheaper brands. Magnetron is the failure point — usually after 7-8 years of heavy use. Look for models with 3-5 year magnetron warranty.

Can I use metal in a microwave?

NO. Never put metal in a microwave — it causes sparks and can damage the magnetron. Use microwave-safe glass, ceramic, or labeled microwave-safe plastic.

What about inverter technology?

Inverter microwaves (Panasonic, some LG) use variable power instead of on/off cycling. Result: more even cooking, faster defrost, lighter weight, slightly more expensive. Worth it for frequent microwave users.

Microwave cooking for Indian food: what works

Microwaves excel at some Indian tasks, struggle with others. Know the difference:

  • Excellent for — Reheating dal, rice, curry (3-4 min on medium). Defrosting frozen meat (5-8 min on defrost mode). Melting ghee/butter. Making poha (5 min). Softening idli batter (3 min). Cooking poha, upma, kheer (10-15 min).
  • Good for — Grilling paneer tikka, chicken tikka (8-12 min on grill mode). Making tandoori roti (4-5 min on convection at 220°C). Roasting papad (1-2 min).
  • Struggles with — Anything that needs real "bhauna" (tempering spices in oil) — the microwave doesn't reach 200°C+ oil temps. Deep frying. Dosa, uttapam, vada — batter-based fried foods.
  • Impossible — Anything requiring pressure (dal, rajma) without a stovetop. Real tandoor-style cooking.

The right mental model: microwave is a powerful reheat + light cooking tool, not a replacement for stovetop. For Indian families who already cook on gas/induction, the microwave saves 30-60 min/day on reheating and light prep.

Convection microwave baking: realistic expectations

Convection microwaves can bake, but the results are not the same as a dedicated OTG (Oven Toaster Griller):

  • What works in convection microwave — Cakes (sponge, chocolate, vanilla), muffins, bread (small loaves), pizza (small), cookies, brownies, garlic bread.
  • What's harder — Croissants, puff pastry (need even heat), large cakes (heat distribution is uneven), artisan bread (needs steam injection).
  • Temperature accuracy — Convection microwaves are typically ±10°C of set temperature. OTGs are ±5°C. For serious baking, OTG is better.

For Indian home bakers who make basic cakes, bread, and cookies occasionally, a convection microwave is enough. For weekly serious baking, pair a convection microwave with a small OTG (₹4,000-6,000 extra) — best of both worlds.

What a microwave adds to your power bill

Microwaves are efficient. A typical Indian household using a microwave 30-45 min/day adds ₹100-150 to monthly electricity bills. Comparison:

  • Microwave (800W) — 30 min/day = 0.4 kWh/day = ₹4/day = ₹120/month (at ₹10/unit)
  • Induction (2000W) — 45 min/day = 1.5 kWh/day = ₹15/day = ₹450/month
  • OTG (2000W) — 20 min/day = 0.67 kWh/day = ₹6.7/day = ₹200/month
  • Gas (LPG) — 1 hour/day = 1 cylinder/month = ₹900-1,100

Microwaves are among the cheapest appliances to run. The 4 picks above all use 800W — comparable efficiency. Convection modes use more power (heater element) but the 5-15 min cooking time keeps total energy low.

First-week setup: what to do when you unbox

Three things to do in the first 24 hours:

  • Remove all packaging and stickers — including the magnetron safety sticker inside. Wipe the cavity with a damp cloth. Don't use harsh chemicals — they leave residue that burns during cooking.
  • Run a 5-minute empty burn-in — microwave a cup of water for 5 minutes on high. This burns off any manufacturing residue. Discard the water, wipe the cavity again. Some new microwaves smell on first use — this is normal.
  • Test every auto-cook menu with water first — the menus are calibrated for specific weights. Put a measured cup of water in, run the menu, see if the result matches expectations. This teaches you how the microwave behaves before cooking real food.

Read the manual cover to cover (15-20 min for most models). The auto-cook menus, defrost timings, and child lock features vary by model. Knowing the unique features saves real time later.

Buy a set of microwave-safe borosilicate glass containers on day one (₹600-1,200 for a 6-piece set). Don't cook in plastic — it leaches chemicals at microwave temperatures. Stainless steel works for reheat but not for microwave mode (blocks the waves).
