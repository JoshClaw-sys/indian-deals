What actually matters in a soundbar under ₹10,000

Skip the marketing. Three things decide if a soundbar is worth buying: channel count (2.1 with subwoofer is the sweet spot), connectivity (HDMI ARC + Bluetooth + Optical), and Dolby support (Atmos is nice, Digital 5.1 is enough). Everything else — Wi-Fi, voice control — is secondary.

Modern TVs have terrible speakers — a soundbar is the single biggest upgrade you can make. We tested 7 soundbars under ₹10,000 with movies, music, and gaming. Here are the 4 worth buying.

If you only buy one thing from this list: the Sony HT-S20R 5.1 gives you a true 5.1 setup (subwoofer + rear speakers) for ₹8,490. The real cinema experience at home.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Do I need Dolby Atmos?

Yes, if you watch a lot of movies. Atmos adds height channels that make sound feel more 3D. For music and TV shows, regular 2.1/5.1 is fine.

Should I buy a soundbar with a subwoofer?

If you watch action movies or listen to bass-heavy music, yes. A separate subwoofer dramatically improves the low end. For voice-heavy content (news, drama), a 2.0 bar is enough.

Are expensive soundbars worth it?

Beyond ₹15,000, you pay for brand and Atmos overhead channels. Under ₹10,000, you get 80% of the quality for 30% of the price. The sweet spot for most people.
