Frequently asked questions

Is 2-ton enough for a 200 sq ft room?

Yes — 2-ton is ideal for rooms between 180–280 sq ft. For a 150 sq ft room, 1.5-ton is enough and saves you ₹6,000–₹8,000. For 300+ sq ft, you should consider 2.5-ton or even 3-ton. Over-tonnaging wastes electricity; under-tonnaging makes the compressor run continuously without cooling.

Split AC vs window AC for 2-ton — which is better?

For 2-ton, split ACs are now cheaper than window ACs at the same capacity. A 2-ton split AC under ₹50,000 is quieter (38–45 dB vs 55–62 dB for window), looks better, and the outdoor unit can be placed away from the bedroom. Window ACs still win on service simplicity and zero installation cost.

Do 2-ton ACs consume a lot more electricity than 1.5-ton?

About 30–40% more, depending on star rating. A 2-ton inverter split AC uses 1.2–1.5 units/hour vs 0.9–1.1 for a 1.5-ton. Running 8 hours a day for 30 days = 290–360 units/month (roughly ₹2,900–₹3,600 at ₹10/unit). A 5-star inverter model cuts this by another 20–25%.

What is the difference between 3-star and 5-star AC?

A 5-star inverter AC uses about 20–25% less electricity than a 3-star inverter AC over a year. The price difference is usually ₹3,000–₹5,000. If you run the AC 6+ hours a day, the 5-star pays back the premium in 18–24 months. For occasional use (2–3 hours/day), the 3-star is the better value.

Do I need a stabilizer for a 2-ton split AC?

Most 2-ton ACs in 2026 from Voltas, LG, and Daikin include wide-voltage inverter compressors that handle 150V–290V without a stabilizer. If your area has voltage drops below 150V or frequent power cuts, a separate 5 kVA stabilizer (₹2,500–₹4,000) is recommended. Always check the model's voltage range before buying.

Is copper condenser really that important?

Yes — copper condensers last 8–12 years even in coastal climates, while aluminum condensers typically corrode in 4–6 years. Copper is also easier to repair (a technician can braze a leak), while aluminum often requires full condenser replacement. The copper version costs ₹1,000–₹2,000 more but pays for itself in longevity.

Should I buy installation as a bundle or separately?

Always buy installation as a bundle with the unit. Most Indian brands include free installation with the AC if you book via their website. Off-brand installation cheap (₹1,500–₹2,500) usually means inexperienced technicians who don't vacuum the lines properly, leading to poor cooling and compressor damage in year 2–3. A proper installation includes copper piping, wall mounting, gas top-up, and electrical work.

Buy the right tonnage for your room. Over-sized ACs short-cycle and waste electricity; under-sized ACs never cool down.
