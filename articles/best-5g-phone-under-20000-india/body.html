What actually matters in a phone under ₹20,000

Skip the megapixel war. Four things decide if a phone is actually good in this segment: processor (Dimensity 7000+/Snapdragon 7+ Gen 2 > older chips), display (AMOLED 120Hz > LCD 60Hz), camera (OIS > non-OIS, sensor size matters more than MP), and battery (5000mAh + 33W+ charging).

We tested 10 phones released in the last 12 months. Here are the 5 worth buying.

If you only buy one thing: the Realme Narzo 70 Pro has the best camera in this segment by a real margin — the OIS + Sony IMX890 sensor is the same hardware as phones twice the price.

One phone to skip

⚠️ Skip

Infinix Zero 30 5G

₹17,999

Despite the spec sheet looking competitive (Dimensity 8020, 108MP camera, 68W charging), the Infinix Zero 30 has a critical issue: software updates. Infinix promises only 1 Android update and 2 years of security patches. For a ₹18,000 phone you'll likely use for 3-4 years, that's a bad deal. The hardware is good, but the software longevity isn't.

The bottom line

For camera: Realme Narzo 70 Pro. For all-round value: Redmi Note 13 Pro. For software longevity: Samsung Galaxy A15. For premium feel: Motorola Edge 40 Neo. For gaming: Poco X6.
