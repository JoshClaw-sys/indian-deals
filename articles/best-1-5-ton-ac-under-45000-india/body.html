Frequently asked questions

Is 1.5 ton enough for my room?

Yes, for rooms between 120 and 180 sq ft. For smaller rooms (under 100 sq ft) get a 1 ton; for larger rooms (200+ sq ft) get a 2 ton. The right size matters more than the brand.

What is ISEER and what is a good rating?

ISEER is the Indian Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio -- higher is more efficient. 5-star ISEER ACs use about 30% less electricity than 3-star. Aim for ISEER 5.0+ in this bracket.

Do I need copper coils?

Yes. Copper coils last longer, cool faster, and are easier to repair than aluminium. Every pick on this list uses copper.

How much electricity does a 1.5 ton AC use?

A 5-star inverter 1.5 ton AC uses about 0.8-1.2 units per hour of active cooling. At ₹7/unit, that's roughly ₹6-9 per hour of use -- or about ₹2,000-3,000 per year if you run it 6 hours daily for 6 months.

Installation and running costs

The advertised price is not the price you'll pay. Budget another ₹3,000-5,000 for installation -- copper piping extension (if the indoor and outdoor units are far apart), wall mounting brackets, drainage pipe, and electrician fees. Most brands offer free installation in the box but, but read the fine print: standard installation is 3 metres of copper piping. Beyond that, it's ₹500-700 per metre.

Annual running cost is the hidden factor. A 5-star 1.5 ton AC used 6 hours daily for 6 months will cost ₹2,000-3,000 in electricity. A 3-star AC will cost ₹2,800-4,200 for the same usage. Over 10 years (the typical compressor life), the 5-star saves ₹8,000-12,000 -- which more than covers the ₹5,000-8,000 price premium.
