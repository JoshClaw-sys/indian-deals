Skip these and you'll regret it within a year.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is ₹80,000 enough for a good gaming laptop in 2026?

Yes. ₹80,000 gets you an RTX 4050 (6GB) or RTX 4060 (8GB) GPU paired with a Ryzen 7 8845HS or Core i7-13650HX. That's enough for 1080p Ultra in most modern AAA titles (60+ FPS) and 1080p Medium/High in demanding games like Cyberpunk 2077.

Is RTX 4050 enough for gaming in 2026?

For 1080p, yes. RTX 4050 delivers 70-90 FPS in esports titles (Valorant, CS2) at max settings and 50-65 FPS in AAA games at high settings with DLSS on. RTX 4060 is the sweet spot if you can stretch — 20-30% faster than 4050 and 8GB VRAM vs 6GB.

Do gaming laptops overheat?

Under sustained load, expect CPU temps of 85-95°C and GPU temps of 78-88°C — that's normal. The real issue is thermal throttling (clock speed drops). Look for laptops with dual fans, 4+ heat pipes, and at least 2 vents (rear + side). Undervolting can drop temps by 10-15°C.

How long do gaming laptops last?

5-7 years for the chassis, 3-5 years before gaming performance feels outdated. Battery degrades after 2-3 years (300-500 cycles). Replace thermal paste at Year 3 to extend life.

Should I buy Intel or AMD for gaming?

AMD Ryzen 7 8845HS / Ryzen 9 8945HS wins on battery and multi-thread. Intel Core i7-13650HX / 14650HX often edges ahead in single-thread and pure gaming FPS (3-8% in most titles). Either is fine — pick by GPU and thermals.
