Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency matters. Indian Deals participates in affiliate programs with Amazon India, Flipkart, and other retailers. This page explains exactly how that works.

What is an affiliate link?

When you click a "Check price on Amazon" button on Indian Deals and buy something, we may receive a small commission (typically 1-6% of the sale). The price you pay is the same — there's no extra cost to you.

Does this bias our recommendations?

No. Our editorial process is the same whether or not a product is in an affiliate program. We:

  • Recommend products we'd genuinely buy ourselves
  • Mark products we recommend skipping with a clear "Skip" badge
  • Update recommendations every 60-90 days based on new models and prices
  • Never accept payment for placement or positive coverage

Which programs do we participate in?

We currently participate in:

  • Amazon India Associates
  • Flipkart Affiliate

Questions?

If you have questions about our affiliate relationships or editorial process, contact us.
