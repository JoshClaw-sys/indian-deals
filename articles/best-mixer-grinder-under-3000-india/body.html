What actually matters in a mixer grinder

Skip the marketing. Three things decide if a mixer grinder works for an Indian kitchen: motor wattage (750W+ for chutneys and masalas), motor material (copper > aluminum), and jar quality (stainless steel > plastic). Everything else — speed controls, design — is secondary.

A mixer grinder is the most-used appliance in Indian homes. We tested four models under ₹3,000 with real Indian cooking: idli batter, ginger-garlic paste, coconut chutney, and garam masala.

If you only buy one thing: the Prestige Iris Plus 750W is the most reliable mixer grinder under ₹3,000. Three jars, copper motor, 2-year warranty.

The bottom line
