What actually matters in an AC under ₹40,000

Skip the marketing. Three things decide if an AC is worth buying in this segment: tonnage (1T for small rooms, 1.5T for medium), inverter vs non-inverter (inverter saves 30-40% power), and star rating (5-star is the sweet spot for power savings). Everything else — Wi-Fi, voice control — is secondary.

Indian summers are unforgiving — a bad AC will ruin your sleep and your electricity bill. We tested six models under ₹40,000 in real use (Delhi summer, 8-hour daily use). Here are the four worth buying.

If you only buy one thing from this list: the LG 1.5 Ton 5 Star Inverter Split AC is the most reliable AC under ₹40,000. AI Convertible mode auto-adjusts cooling based on room conditions, and LG's service network is unmatched.

Pick the right tonnage for your room

Most people over-buy or under-buy tonnage. Here's the right rule:

  • 1 ton — rooms up to 100 sq ft (small bedroom, study, small office)
  • 1.5 ton — 100-180 sq ft (master bedroom, medium living room) — most common
  • 2 ton — 180+ sq ft OR rooms with direct sun exposure for >4 hours

Wrong tonnage = bad cooling OR wasted electricity. 1.5 ton is the right answer for 70% of Indian homes.
