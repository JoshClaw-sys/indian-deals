What actually matters in a geyser under ₹10,000

Storage or instant is the first decision. Five things decide which ₹10,000 geyser is worth buying: capacity (3L instant for kitchen, 10-15L for bathroom, 25L for baths), type (storage vs instant — storage uses more power but delivers steady hot water), wattage (2KW for instant, 2-3KW for storage), tank material (BEE 5-star rated glass-lined tanks last longer in hard water), and warranty (tank warranty is the real number — 5-7 years is the standard). For Indian conditions — hard water, voltage fluctuations, occasional dry heating — brand reliability matters more than features.

For most Indian homes, a 15L storage geyser with a 5-star BEE rating and 5-year tank warranty is the right shape. Below 10L is too small for a bath. Above 25L wastes electricity for daily use.

If you only buy one thing from this list: the Crompton Solarium Qube 15L (SWH215) — best balance of 5-star BEE rating, glass-lined tank, magnesium anode for hard water, and 5-year tank warranty at ₹7,500.

Storage vs instant geyser: which to buy

Two distinct use cases, two distinct products. Common confusion — they do different things:

  • Storage geyser (10-25L) — Heats 10-25L and stores it. Gives 8-15 minutes of hot water for a bath. Best for bathrooms. Takes 5-15 min to heat up. Uses 2-3KW power. Higher electricity per use but steady output.
  • Instant geyser (1-3L) — Heats water on demand. Best for kitchen (washing dishes, hand-wash). 5-10 min of hot water for a single use. Takes 30-60 sec to heat. Uses 3-4.5KW power. Lower per-use but limited flow.

For most Indian homes: storage geyser in the bathroom (15L for a family of 4) + instant geyser in the kitchen (3L). Don't use an instant in the bathroom — 3L isn't enough for a bucket bath.

Frequently asked questions

Storage or instant geyser — which is better?

Storage (10-25L) for bathroom — gives 8-15 min of hot water for a bath. Instant (1-3L) for kitchen — gives 5-10 min for washing dishes. Don't use instant in bathroom (3L is not enough for a bucket bath). Don't use storage in kitchen (15L is overkill for hand-wash).

How long does a geyser take to heat water?

15L storage: 8-12 minutes. 10L storage: 5-8 minutes. 3L instant: 30-60 seconds. 1L instant: 10-20 seconds. The 4 storage picks above all heat 15L in 10-12 minutes at 2000W.

What's the best BEE rating for a geyser?

5-star is the highest. 5-star geysers use 15-20% less electricity than 4-star at the same capacity. The difference on a monthly bill is ₹50-100 for a family of 4. Worth the ₹500-1,000 upfront premium.

How long does a geyser last?

8-12 years for AO Smith, Crompton, Havells, Bajaj. 5-7 years for V-Guard and cheaper brands. The tank is the failure point — usually from corrosion in hard water. Magnesium anode extends tank life by 2-3 years. Look for 5-7 year tank warranty.

Horizontal or vertical mounting?

Vertical is standard for storage geysers (90% of installations). Horizontal is for some special installations (low ceiling, narrow wall) but is less common. Most brands offer vertical only at this price. The 4 picks above are vertical mount.

Hard water and geyser longevity

Hard water is the silent killer of geysers in India. Calcium and magnesium deposits accumulate on the heating element and tank walls, reducing efficiency and shortening life. Three things matter:

  • Magnesium anode — Sacrificial metal rod that attracts corrosion before the tank does. Replaced every 3-4 years (₹200-400). All 4 picks above have magnesium anode.
  • Glass-lined tank — Borosilicate glass lining on the inside of the tank prevents corrosion. Standard on all 4 picks. Look for "Blue Diamond" or "Feroglas" branding for premium lining.
  • Heating element material — Incoloy heating element (AO Smith, some Crompton) resists corrosion better than standard copper. Premium geysers use Incoloy.

For cities with hard water (Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune), all three of the above are non-negotiable. For soft water cities (Mumbai, parts of Kerala), regular geysers work fine but the same premium features are still worth the small premium.

What a geyser adds to your power bill

Storage geysers are the highest electricity-use appliance in most Indian homes. Quick math:

  • 15L storage, 5-star — 15L × 1 unit heat = 0.83 kWh per bath. 4 baths/day = 3.3 kWh/day = ₹33/day = ₹1,000/month.
  • 15L storage, 4-star — 15L × 1.15 unit heat = 0.95 kWh per bath. 4 baths/day = 3.8 kWh/day = ₹38/day = ₹1,150/month.
  • 10L storage, 5-star — 10L × 1 unit heat = 0.55 kWh per bath. 4 baths/day = 2.2 kWh/day = ₹22/day = ₹660/month.
  • 3L instant, 4.5KW — 3L × 1 unit heat = 0.17 kWh per use. 10 uses/day = 1.7 kWh/day = ₹17/day = ₹510/month.

5-star vs 4-star on a 15L storage geyser saves ₹150-200/month. Over 10 years, that's ₹18,000-24,000. The premium for 5-star is ₹500-1,000 upfront — easily worth it. Solar water heaters (₹12,000-25,000) eliminate this entirely but require rooftop installation.

Installation and safety

Three things to do before installing:

  • Use a 15-amp socket — Geysers draw 2000W. A standard 5-amp socket or extension cord will overheat. Use a dedicated 15-amp socket with a heavy-gauge wire (2.5mm² minimum).
  • Install a 16-amp MCB — Protects against short circuits. Combine with RCCB (residual current circuit breaker) for shock protection. Both are mandatory by code (and your insurance won't cover accidents without them).
  • Mount vertically on a strong wall — A 15L storage geyser weighs 11-13kg when full. Use the wall plugs that come with the unit, not generic ones. Brick walls need different plugs than RCC walls.

Don't install a geyser yourself. Use a licensed plumber/electrician. The brand's installation service is free for most premium brands (Crompton, Havells, AO Smith) — use it. Poor installation causes 80% of geyser failures in the first year.

Set the thermostat to 55-60°C. Higher temperatures scale the tank faster and waste electricity. Lower temperatures aren't hot enough for comfortable bathing in winter. 55-60°C is the sweet spot for Indian conditions.
