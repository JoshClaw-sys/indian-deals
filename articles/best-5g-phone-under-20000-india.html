What actually matters in a phone under ₹20,000

Skip the megapixel war. Four things decide if a phone is actually good in this segment: processor (Dimensity 7000+/Snapdragon 7+ Gen 2 > older chips), display (AMOLED 120Hz > LCD 60Hz), camera (OIS > non-OIS, sensor size matters more than MP), and battery (5000mAh + 33W+ charging).

We tested 10 phones released in the last 12 months. Here are the 5 worth buying.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is MediaTek Dimensity good?

Yes — the Dimensity 7000 and 8000 series compete head-to-head with Qualcomm Snapdragon 7-series. The 1080 and 6100+ chips are also solid for the price.

How much storage do I need?

128GB minimum. 256GB is better if you shoot a lot of video. Always check for microSD support — many budget phones skip it.

Which phone has the best camera under ₹20,000?

Realme Narzo 70 Pro and Redmi Note 13 Pro have the best main cameras in this segment. For selfies, Samsung Galaxy A15 leads.
