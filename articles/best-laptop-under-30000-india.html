What actually matters in a laptop under ₹30,000

The best laptop under ₹30,000 in 2026 is not the one with the biggest screen or the loudest spec sheet. It is the one that boots in 15 seconds, opens Chrome quickly, and lasts 6+ hours on battery during a college day. Skip the marketing claims and focus on CPU (Ryzen 3 7000-series or Intel i3 12th gen+), RAM (8GB minimum), storage (SSD, not HDD), and battery (real 6+ hours).

Two hardware decisions dominate this segment. First, an SSD or HDD: an SSD makes the laptop feel 3-5x faster in daily use. A 1TB HDD laptop looks attractive on the spec sheet, but every boot, every app launch, every file copy will test your patience. Second, the CPU generation: a Ryzen 3 7320U is meaningfully faster and more efficient than a Ryzen 3 5300U, and an i3-1215U is faster than an i3-1115G4. The 2-generation gap matters.

Under ₹30,000 you will not get a dedicated GPU, a 16GB RAM option, or a 1TB SSD. Accept that — and instead pick a laptop that does the basics well. The picks below are the models we would buy for our own siblings starting college in 2026.

If you only buy one thing from this list: get the Acer Aspire 3 with the Ryzen 3 7320U, 8GB DDR5 and 512GB SSD. It is the most balanced under ₹30,000 — current AMD silicon, fast DDR5 RAM, roomy SSD, and a battery that genuinely lasts a college day.

The 2026 sweet spot — what to look for

At this budget the right spec sheet in 2026 is: AMD Ryzen 3 7320U / 7430U OR Intel i3-1215U / i3-1305U, 8GB DDR4 or DDR5, 256GB or 512GB SSD, 15.6-inch Full HD display (1920x1080). Anything older than a 12th-gen Intel or a 5000-series AMD Ryzen is dated. Anything with 4GB RAM is too slow for Windows 11. Anything with an HDD is too slow for daily use.

RAM — 8GB is the floor

4GB laptops exist at this budget. They will boot Windows 11 and run one or two apps. As soon as you open Chrome with five tabs, MS Word, and Zoom, they crawl. Buy 8GB minimum. If a 4GB model is heavily discounted, check if it has an empty SO-DIMM slot for a later upgrade — most do.

Display — FHD is non-negotiable

1366x768 (HD) displays are still on the market in 2026 at this price. They look pixelated for text, and most apps assume a 1920x1080 panel. Spend the extra ₹2,000-₹4,000 for a Full HD display. Your eyes will thank you during 6-hour study sessions.

Battery — marketing vs real use

Brands quote 8-12 hours. Real use with Wi-Fi, Chrome and 50% brightness is closer to 5-7 hours. AMD Ryzen laptops consistently beat Intel i3 on battery in this segment by 1-1.5 hours. If battery is critical for campus life, prefer Ryzen.

For ₹1,000-₹2,000 more, the Acer Aspire 3 gives you a current Ryzen 3 7320U, DDR5 RAM and a 512GB SSD. Spend the extra — you will use this laptop for 4-5 years.

Avoid the HP 15s with the 1TB HDD. The HDD is not worth saving ₹1,000-₹2,000 over an SSD laptop.

Frequently asked questions

Is a laptop under ₹30,000 worth buying in 2026?

Yes, for students, online classes, office work, and browsing. Skip gaming and video editing at this budget — those need ₹50,000+. Look for at least 8GB RAM, an SSD, and a current-gen Intel i3 or AMD Ryzen 3.

What RAM is enough at this price?

8GB is the minimum for Windows 11. 4GB laptops crawl when Chrome has 5+ tabs open. If a 4GB model is on sale, verify it has an empty RAM slot.

SSD vs HDD — does it really matter?

Massively. A 256GB SSD boots in 12-15 seconds; a 1TB HDD laptop takes 60-90 seconds. App launch and file copy are 3-5x faster on SSD. Never buy an HDD-only laptop in 2026.

Intel i3 vs AMD Ryzen 3 at ₹30,000?

AMD Ryzen 3 wins on multi-core and battery. Intel i3 (12th/13th gen) wins on single-core and software compatibility. For students and office work, both are fine.

How long should the battery last?

6-8 hours of real use is the realistic floor for campus life. Anything below 5 hours means carrying the charger everywhere.

Buy for the storage type, not the size

A 256GB SSD laptop feels faster every day than a 1TB HDD laptop. Pick the SSD model, then add an external drive or cloud storage if you need more space.
