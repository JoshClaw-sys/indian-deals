What actually matters in a phone under ₹40,000

Three years ago, ₹40,000 bought you last year's flagship. In 2026, it buys a phone that is barely distinguishable from ₹65,000+ flagships -- Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, dedicated telephoto lenses, 100W+ charging and 5+ years of software support. The catch: every phone in this bracket looks great on paper, but only a handful actually deliver on the marketing.

We picked six that do. Here's what we looked at, and which popular model we recommend skipping.

The five specs that decide if a ₹40,000 phone is worth the premium over a ₹25,000 phone: Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 or Dimensity 9200+ chipset, dedicated telephoto lens with 2x or 3x optical zoom, IP67 or IP68 water resistance, 100W+ bundled charger, and 5+ years of OS updates from the manufacturer. AMOLED 120Hz is standard in this segment.

If you only buy one thing from this list: get the OnePlus 13R. Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, 50 MP main + 50 MP 2x telephoto, 100W charging and 5500 mAh battery. At ₹39,999, it is the most balanced near-flagship under ₹40,000 in India right now.

Frequently asked questions

Is a ₹40,000 phone better than a ₹25,000 phone in 2026?

For most buyers -- not by much. The ₹25,000 segment already gives you AMOLED 120Hz, 5G and a competent camera. The ₹40,000 segment adds telephoto cameras, faster Snapdragon 8-series chipsets, longer software support and IP ratings. If your current phone is two years old and you take a lot of zoom shots, the upgrade is worth it. Otherwise, save the ₹15,000 and grab a ₹25,000 phone.

Which chipset should I look for under ₹40,000?

Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, Snapdragon 8s Gen 3, or Dimensity 9200+. Anything older (Snapdragon 7-series, Dimensity 8000-series) feels sluggish by 2027 standards. The OnePlus 13R (Snapdragon 8 Gen 3), iQOO Neo 9 Pro (Dimensity 9300) and Realme GT 6 (Snapdragon 8s Gen 3) are the chipset winners in this bracket.

How important is a telephoto lens under ₹40,000?

Important if you take zoom shots at concerts, weddings or while travelling. Phones with a dedicated 3x telephoto (OnePlus 13R 2x, Vivo V30 Pro 2x, Samsung S23 FE 3x) produce dramatically better 3x-10x zoom than phones that crop from the main sensor. For everyday shots under 2x, the main camera is plenty.

Should I buy the Pixel 8a or wait for the Pixel 9a?

Buy the Pixel 8a now. It already has Tensor G3, 7 years of OS support (until 2031), and IP67 water resistance at ₹37,999. The Pixel 9a may launch in October 2026 with marginal upgrades but at a ₹45,000+ price. The 8a hits the sweet spot for stock Android.

Which phone under ₹40,000 has the cleanest software?

Pixel 8a (stock Android, 7 years of updates), OnePlus 13R (OxygenOS, 4 years of updates), and Samsung Galaxy S23 FE (One UI, 5 years of updates). Avoid Realme UI, FunTouch OS and HyperOS in this bracket if software longevity matters to you.

The bottom line

If you want the most balanced phone under ₹40,000 in India, the OnePlus 13R at ₹39,999 is the right pick -- Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, telephoto camera, 100W charging and 4 years of updates.
