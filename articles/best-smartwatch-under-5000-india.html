What actually matters in a budget smartwatch

Skip the marketing. Four things decide if a budget smartwatch is worth buying in 2026: display (AMOLED > LCD), GPS (built-in > connected), battery (7+ days), and sensor accuracy (heart rate + SpO2).

Two years ago, all of these features cost ₹10,000+. Now you can get them under ₹5,000. We tested six watches in this segment.

If you only buy one thing: the Amazfit Bip 5 has the best battery (14 days), built-in GPS, and AMOLED display — all under ₹5,000. The closest competition.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Do budget smartwatches have GPS?

Yes — many sub-₹5,000 watches now have built-in GPS (not just connected GPS). Look for 'built-in GPS' specifically if you want accurate run tracking.

AMOLED vs LCD — does it matter?

Massively. AMOLED is brighter outdoors, has true blacks, and uses less battery when displaying dark watch faces. Always pick AMOLED in 2026.
