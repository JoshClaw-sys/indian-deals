What actually matters in a storage geyser under Rs 15,000

A storage water heater is one of the most-purchased appliances in Indian homes during the October-January winter months, and the 2026 lineup has stratified enough that the wrong choice is usually picking a non-glass-coat tank in a hard-water city or a 3-star geyser when a 5-star model pays back its premium in 6-9 months. In 2026, the categories have stratified by tank size (10L vs 15L vs 25L vs 35L+), BEE star rating (5-star vs 3-star vs unrated), and glass-coat lining (titanium-enamel vs standard steel).

The four things that actually move the needle on an Indian storage geyser in 2026 are tank capacity, BEE star rating, glass-coat lining, and tank warranty length. Brand matters too, but the four major brands in this list (Racold, AO Smith, Crompton, Havells, Bajaj) all have reachable service networks -- the gap is in tank lining quality and warranty length.

If you only buy one thing from this list: the Racold Omnis 25L Storage Water Heater. It has a 25L tank capacity for a 4-person family, BEE 5-star rating, titanium-enamel glass-coat lining for hard-water durability, and Racold's 7-year tank warranty.

Tank size: 25L is the right pick for a 4-person family

A 10L geyser works for a single-person household with one bath; a 15L works for a 2-person household with one bath at a time. The 25L class is the sweet spot for a 4-person Indian family with morning bath rush -- it handles two consecutive showers without reheat downtime. 35L+ is right for large families (5-6 people) or homes where two bathrooms share the same geyser. Going below 25L for a 4-person family means waiting 15-25 minutes between showers; going above 25L wastes electricity heating water you don't use.

BEE star rating: 5-star saves Rs 2,000-3,500 per year

BEE (Bureau of Energy Efficiency) star rating is the Indian government's energy-efficiency label for storage water heaters. A 5-star geyser consumes 2-2.5 kWh per day vs 3.5-4 kWh for a 3-star geyser. Over a year of daily use, a 5-star geyser saves about 400-500 units of electricity per year vs a 3-star geyser. At a subsidized domestic tariff of Rs 5-7 per unit, that translates to Rs 2,000-3,500 in annual savings. The geyser pays back its Rs 1,000-1,500 premium for 5-star rating in 6-9 months of ownership.

Glass-coat lining: essential for hard-water cities

Glass-coat lining (also called vitreous enamel or titanium-enamel coating) is a glass-based inner-tank coating that protects the steel tank from corrosion by hard water. Hard water (high calcium/magnesium content) destroys standard steel tanks in 3-5 years, causing leaks and tank failure. Cities like Delhi, Jaipur, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru have hard water. A glass-coat lined tank lasts 8-12 years; a non-lined steel tank in hard water lasts 3-5 years. Always pick a glass-coat lined tank for hard-water cities.

Tank warranty: 7 years is the real signal

Brand-published tank warranties range from 1 year (sub-Rs 6,500 marketplace listings) to 7 years (Racold Omnis, AO Smith HSE-SHS series). The tank is the most expensive component to replace, and a 7-year tank warranty means the brand is willing to back the tank's reliability for daily Indian usage over 7 winters. Sub-Rs 6,500 listings usually publish 1-2 years and have no service network after the warranty expires. The heating element warranty is typically 2-4 years; the tank warranty is the more important number to track.

Frequently asked questions

What tank size do I need for my family?

10L works for a single-person household with one bath. 15L works for a 2-person household with one bath at a time. 25L is the sweet spot for a 4-person Indian family with morning bath rush. 35L+ is right for large families (5-6 people) or homes where two bathrooms share the same geyser. Going below 25L for a 4-person family means waiting 15-25 minutes between showers; going above 25L wastes electricity heating water you don't use.

How long does a 25L geyser take to heat?

A 25L geyser with a 2KW heating element takes about 12-18 minutes to heat from 25 degrees C ambient to 60 degrees C, depending on inlet water temperature. Winter in North India (10-15 degrees C inlet) takes closer to 18-22 minutes. Summer in South India (28-32 degrees C inlet) takes about 8-12 minutes. Higher-wattage 3KW elements reduce this by 30-40% but require a separate 15A socket and dedicated MCB.

What is BEE star rating for geysers and why does it matter?

BEE (Bureau of Energy Efficiency) star rating is the Indian government's energy-efficiency label for storage water heaters. A 5-star geyser consumes 2-2.5 kWh per day vs 3.5-4 kWh for a 3-star geyser. Over a year of daily use, a 5-star geyser saves about 400-500 units of electricity per year vs a 3-star geyser. At a subsidized domestic tariff of Rs 5-7 per unit, that translates to Rs 2,000-3,500 in annual savings. The geyser pays back its Rs 1,000-1,500 premium for 5-star rating in 6-9 months of ownership.

What is glass-coat lining and why does it matter?

Glass-coat lining (also called vitreous enamel or titanium-enamel coating) is a glass-based inner-tank coating that protects the steel tank from corrosion by hard water. Hard water (high calcium/magnesium content) destroys standard steel tanks in 3-5 years, causing leaks and tank failure. Cities like Delhi, Jaipur, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru have hard water. A glass-coat lined tank lasts 8-12 years; a non-lined steel tank in hard water lasts 3-5 years. Always pick a glass-coat lined tank for hard-water cities.

Can a storage geyser work with a normal home power socket?

Most 2KW storage geysers work with a normal 5A socket, but the recommended setup is a dedicated 15A socket with a separate MCB. A 3KW geyser REQUIRES a 15A socket and dedicated MCB -- using a normal 5A socket will trip the MCB on first use. Most modern geysers come with a 3-pin plug compatible with a 15A socket. If your bathroom doesn't have a 15A socket, have an electrician install one before installing the geyser.
